Class Chat Guidelines

A quick read so the room stays friendly for everyone.

  • Be kind. No bullying, name-calling or personal attacks — ever.
  • Keep it clean. No abusive, rude or inappropriate language or photos.
  • Stay on topic. This room is for class work: homework, doubts, papers and notes.
  • Keep private things private. Don't share anyone's phone number, address or password.
  • No spam. Avoid repeated messages, forward chains and unnecessary links.
  • Admin can block. Breaking these rules can get your account blocked, with a reason and a duration.

By continuing you agree to follow these rules. Everyone can read the class chat, so write as if the whole class is listening.
